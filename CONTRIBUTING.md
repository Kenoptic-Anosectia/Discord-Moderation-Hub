# Contributing to the Discord Moderation Hub
Thanks for helping improve the Hub. You don't need to be an experienced moderator to contribute. Fixing a broken link, clarifying a guide, or sharing a template that worked for your team are all welcome.
## What you can contribute
- Moderation guides, onboarding materials, checklists, and templates.
- Proposals for better rules, reporting procedures, enforcement, or appeals.
- Bot recommendations, configuration examples, and accessibility improvements.
- Corrections to outdated, unclear, or inaccurate resources.
- Training scenarios based on fictional or thoroughly anonymized examples.
## How to submit a contribution
1. Search the repository and its existing issues and pull requests for similar work.
2. Open an **issue** to flag a problem or discuss an idea. For larger changes, especially policy proposals, start with an issue before drafting a pull request.
3. Open a **pull request** when you have a specific change ready to review. Explain what changed and why, link any related issue, and note how you checked the change. Small fixes can go straight to a pull request.
4. If you're contributing through Discord instead, use the appropriate Hub channel or forum tag.
## Protect privacy and confidentiality
> [!IMPORTANT]
> GitHub issues, pull requests, and commit history are not places to share moderation evidence. Use the designated private reporting or appeals process for active cases, complaints, and appeals.
- Don't post private messages, internal moderation discussions, reports, or case records without appropriate permission. For public examples, prefer fictional scenarios.
- Check screenshots, attachments, links, and diffs for usernames, user IDs, avatars, invite links, and other identifying details. Removing names alone may not make a case anonymous.
- Never include passwords, bot tokens, API keys, or other credentials. Use placeholders in configuration examples.
- Describe harmful or prohibited material without uploading or reproducing it. If you're unsure whether something can be shared, ask a Hub administrator privately before posting.
## Keep discussions constructive
Critique the wording, policy, or proposed action rather than the person behind it. Explain your reasoning, acknowledge trade-offs, and remember that a practice that works in one server may not fit another.
Do not use contribution threads for public accusations, callouts, gossip, or speculation about someone's motives. Do not encourage harassment, retaliation, raids, or ban evasion.
## Make resources easy to use
- Use clear language, descriptive headings, and examples a moderator can follow. Note prerequisites and limitations where they matter.
- Give screenshots meaningful alt text and explain any important details in the surrounding text.
- Cite sources for factual claims and distinguish them from personal experience or recommendations. Credit borrowed material and respect its license or permission requirements.
- Disclose relevant affiliations when recommending a bot, product, or service.
- Review AI-assisted contributions for accuracy, privacy, and originality. You're responsible for what you submit.
## Proposing a policy change
Include the following in your issue:
- **Problem:** What isn't working, and who is affected?
- **Proposed change:** What wording or process would you use instead?
- **Trade-offs:** What would improve? What could go wrong or be misused?
- **Implementation:** What would moderators need to apply the change consistently?
Where relevant, explain how the proposal handles accessibility, proportionate enforcement, and appeals.
## Review and follow-up
Maintainers may ask for edits, clarification, or supporting evidence. Give them time to respond and keep follow-up in the existing issue or pull request. If a proposal is declined, you can ask for clarification respectfully. If you revise it, explain how you addressed the feedback.
A proposal is not official Hub guidance until the Hub team approves it. If an approved resource becomes outdated, open an issue or propose a correction and explain what changed.
